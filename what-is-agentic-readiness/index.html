What Is Agentic Readiness?

A plain-English guide: what it means, why it matters now, how it differs from SEO and GEO, and how it’s measured.

Definition

Agentic readiness

Agentic readiness is how prepared a business is for AI agents: assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity that research, recommend and increasingly act on a customer’s behalf. A business with high agentic readiness can be reached by AI crawlers, is understood correctly, is named when customers ask for a recommendation, publishes machine-readable data, and can take an enquiry or order through an agent.

“Agentic” refers to AI agents: software that doesn’t just answer a question but carries out a task. When a customer asks an assistant to find an accountant, compare insurance or reorder supplies, the agent decides which businesses to mention and, increasingly, which one to use.

The phrase is also used for an organisation’s internal readiness to deploy AI agents. The Agentic Readiness Score is about the outward side: how ready your business is to be found, understood and chosen by the agents your customers use.

Why it matters now

A search engine shows a list and lets the customer choose. An AI assistant usually returns two or three names with a reason for each. Businesses that aren’t named don’t get a second chance on that question.

AI answers also vary from one run to the next, which is why a single spot check tells you very little. Measuring agentic readiness properly means asking the same questions many times and counting.

Common questions

Is agentic readiness the same as AI readiness?

Not quite. “AI readiness” usually means an organisation’s readiness to adopt AI internally. Agentic readiness, as measured here, is outward-facing: whether AI agents acting for customers can find, understand, recommend and transact with your business.

Is agentic readiness the same as GEO?

No. Generative engine optimisation is a set of practices for improving how AI answers use your content. Agentic readiness is the state you’re trying to improve, and the score is how you measure it.

What is agentic commerce?

Buying and selling where an AI agent does some of the work for the customer: researching, comparing, filling the cart or placing the order. The Transaction Readiness pillar measures whether you can take part.

What is a good agentic readiness score?

60 and above (Visible or Leading) means AI assistants regularly name and describe you. The most useful comparison is with businesses of the same type in your market.
