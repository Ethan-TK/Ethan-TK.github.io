What is agentic readiness?

A plain-English guide to agentic readiness: what it means, why it matters now, how it differs from SEO and GEO, and how it is measured.

Definition

Agentic readiness

Agentic readiness is how prepared a business is for AI agents: assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity that research, recommend and increasingly act on a customer’s behalf. A business with high agentic readiness can be reached by AI crawlers, is described accurately, is named when customers ask for a recommendation, publishes machine-readable data, and can take an enquiry or order through an agent.

“Agentic” refers to AI agents: software that doesn’t just answer a question but carries out a task for someone. When a customer asks an assistant to find an accountant, compare insurance or reorder supplies, the agent decides which businesses to mention and, increasingly, which one to use.

The phrase is also used for an organisation’s internal readiness to deploy AI agents. The Agentic Readiness Score is about the outward side: how ready your business is to be found, understood and chosen by the agents your customers use.

Why it matters now

A search engine shows a list and lets the customer choose. An AI assistant usually returns two or three names with a reason for each. Businesses that aren’t named don’t get a second chance to compete on that question.

AI answers also vary from one run to the next. A business can be recommended in one conversation and missing from the next, which is why a single spot check tells you very little. Measuring agentic readiness properly means asking the same questions many times and counting.

The five parts of agentic readiness

PartThe question it answers
Technical accessCan AI crawlers reach and read your website, or are they blocked?
Content clarityCan an AI understand, quote and summarise your content correctly?
Brand commandDo AI assistants name you unprompted, and describe you accurately and positively?
Structured dataAre your products, prices, services and details published in a form machines can use?
Transaction readinessCould an AI agent enquire, book or buy from you on a customer’s behalf?

How much each part counts depends on the business. An online store depends on product data and checkout; a law firm depends on reputation. The score adjusts for this with business-type profiles, so you are compared fairly.

Agentic readiness compared with SEO and GEO

What it isWhat it tells you
SEOImproving how you rank in search engine resultsWhere you appear in a list of links
GEO and AEOPractices for improving how AI-generated answers use your contentWhat to change in your content
Internal AI readinessAn organisation’s preparedness to adopt AI itselfWhether your teams, data and systems can use AI
Agentic readinessHow ready you are to be found, recommended and used by AI agentsWhether AI names you today, and why or why not

These overlap. Strong SEO helps AI assistants find you, and GEO work can lift your score. The difference is that agentic readiness is measured from the customer’s side of the conversation: what the AI actually says.

How agentic readiness is measured

The Agentic Readiness Score puts 19 customer questions to AI assistants, from first need to choosing a provider. Each question is asked five times, with web search on and again with it off, and every answer is recorded. Mention rates, sentiment and the gap between search-on and search-off results are combined with checks of your website and data into a score from 0 to 100.

Common questions

Is agentic readiness the same as AI readiness?

Not quite. “AI readiness” usually means an organisation’s readiness to adopt AI internally: data, skills, governance. Agentic readiness, as measured by the Agentic Readiness Score, is outward-facing: whether AI agents acting for customers can find, understand, recommend and transact with your business.

Is agentic readiness the same as GEO?

No. Generative engine optimisation is a set of practices for improving how AI answers use your content. Agentic readiness is the state you are trying to improve, and the score is how you measure it.

What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce is buying and selling where an AI agent does some of the work for the customer: researching options, comparing prices, filling the cart or placing the order. Transaction readiness, one of the five parts of the score, measures whether your business can take part.

What is a good agentic readiness score?

Scores run from 0 to 100 in five bands. 60 and above (Visible or Leading) means AI assistants regularly name and describe you. Because businesses differ, the most useful comparison is with others of the same type in your market.

How do you measure agentic readiness?

We ask AI assistants a fixed set of 19 customer questions, five times each, with web search on and off, and record whether and how your business is named. Those results are combined with checks on your website and data. The full method is on the methodology page.
