Agentic readiness checker

Enter your website to check how ready it is for AI agents. We read your home page, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap and score four pillars in about a minute.

Pages are fetched through a fetch service and nothing is stored.

Results

What the check looks at

23 checks across the four pillars that can be read from your website. Each pillar is scored from 0 to 100, and the quick check score is a weighted combination of them, with Technical Access counting most.

P1 Technical Access

  • Secure connection. The site loads over HTTPS.
  • robots.txt. A robots.txt file exists and states your rules.
  • AI crawler access. Nine AI crawlers, from ChatGPT search to Common Crawl, are checked one by one against your robots.txt.
  • Indexing allowed. Nothing on the home page tells crawlers not to index it.
  • XML sitemap. A sitemap is declared or published, so every page can be found.

P2 Content Extractability

  • Readable without JavaScript. How much text is in the HTML before scripts run.
  • Page title and description. Clear, well-sized title and meta description.
  • Heading structure. One main heading and clear section headings.
  • Language declared. The page states its language.
  • llms.txt summary. A plain-text guide to your business written for AI tools.
  • Image descriptions. Meaningful images have alt text.
  • Canonical URL. The preferred address for the page is declared.

P4 Data Liquidity

  • Structured data present. Valid schema.org JSON-LD on the page.
  • Organisation details. Name, url, logo, address, phone and profile links in schema.
  • Open Graph tags. Title, description and image for link previews.
  • Offers and answers described. Product, Service, Offer, FAQ or breadcrumb schema.
  • Contact details machine-readable. Phone, email or address in structured data.

P5 Transaction Readiness

  • Clear next step. Links and buttons to buy, book, enquire or start.
  • Ways to get in touch. Phone links, email links and forms.
  • Prices visible. Prices or ranges in the page text.
  • Prices in structured data. Offers with price and currency.
  • Actions described for agents. schema.org actions such as ReserveAction or OrderAction.

P3 Brand Command

Not included. Whether AI assistants name you has to be measured by asking them, which is what the full rating does.

Limits of a quick check

  • It reads your home page only. Other pages may do better or worse.
  • It reads the HTML your server sends, without running JavaScript, the same way many AI crawlers do.
  • Bot-blocking set at your CDN or firewall may not show in robots.txt.
  • Pillars are weighted, with Technical Access counting most. The full rating weights them for your type of business.

Questions

Is the check really free?

Yes. There’s no sign-up and nothing to install.

Do you store my results?

No. The check runs in your browser. Pages are fetched through a fetch service because browsers can’t read other websites directly; results aren’t saved.

Why did the check fail on my site?

Some sites block automated requests, or take too long to respond. That doesn’t affect your score, and the full rating doesn’t depend on it.

Is this my Agentic Readiness Score?

No. It’s a quick check of four pillars from your home page, weighted, with Technical Access counting most. The full score adds Brand Command, looks beyond the home page, weights the pillars for your type of business and compares you with your market.
