The confidence ratio

With web search on, an assistant can look you up in the moment. With it off, it relies on what it learned in training. A business named both ways is part of the assistant’s knowledge. A business named only with search is easy to lose.

Confidence ratio = mention rate with web search ÷ mention rate without

RatioWhat it means
1.5 or belowStrong. Known from memory.
Above 1.5, up to 3Moderate. Partly relies on search.
Above 3Weak. Mainly found through search.
No mentions without searchSearch-dependent. Invisible from memory.

Weighted for the kind of business you run

An online store needs great product data so AI can recommend its products. A law firm needs authority and trust. So the score uses a weighting profile for your type of business: e-commerce, professional services, local business, software and technology, media and publishing, or a general profile where all five pillars count equally.

  • E-commerce: Data Liquidity and Transaction Readiness count most.
  • Professional services and local businesses: Brand Command counts most.
  • Media and publishing: Content Extractability counts most.
  • Software and technology: broadly balanced, with Content Extractability important.

Businesses are compared with others on the same profile. Read the methodology.

Five bands, from Invisible to Leading

  • Leading 80–100A default recommendation in its category.
  • Visible 60–79Regularly named and described accurately.
  • Emerging 40–59Known, but often passed over.
  • Low visibility 20–39Occasionally named, rarely recommended.
  • Invisible 0–19AI assistants don’t know it exists.
