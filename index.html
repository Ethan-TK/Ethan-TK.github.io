Why one AI answer isn’t enough

Ask an AI assistant the same question twice and you’ll often get different answers. A business can be recommended in one conversation and missing from the next, so a single spot check tells you very little.

The full rating asks every question five times, then again with the assistant’s web search switched off, to separate what it looks up from what it already knows. That gap is the confidence ratio.

Questions

What is agentic readiness?

Agentic readiness is how prepared a business is for AI agents: assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity that research, recommend and increasingly act on a customer’s behalf. A business with high agentic readiness can be reached by AI crawlers, is understood correctly, is named when customers ask for a recommendation, publishes machine-readable data, and can take an enquiry or order through an agent.

What does the free check look at?

It reads your home page, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap, and runs 23 checks across four pillars: Technical Access, Content Extractability, Data Liquidity and Transaction Readiness. It takes about a minute and needs no sign-up.

Why isn’t Brand Command in the free check?

Brand Command is whether AI assistants actually name you. That can’t be read from your website; it has to be measured by asking assistants customer questions, many times over. That’s what the full rating does.

How is the full Agentic Readiness Score worked out?

AI assistants are asked 19 customer questions across discovery, evaluation and transaction, five times each, with web search on and off. Those results are combined with the four website pillars, weighted for your type of business, into a score from 0 to 100.

How is this different from SEO or GEO?

SEO is about ranking in a list of search results; GEO and AEO are practices for improving how AI answers use your content. The Agentic Readiness Score is a measurement of where you stand, so you can tell whether that work is paying off.
