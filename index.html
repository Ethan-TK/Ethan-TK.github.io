What is agentic readiness?

AI assistants are becoming the first place people ask for advice, and they are starting to act for them: comparing providers, booking, buying. Agentic readiness is whether your business is ready for that.

Agentic readiness noun
Agentic readiness is how prepared a business is for AI agents: assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity that research, recommend and increasingly act on a customer’s behalf. A business with high agentic readiness can be reached by AI crawlers, is described accurately, is named when customers ask for a recommendation, publishes machine-readable data, and can take an enquiry or order through an agent.
The term is also used for an organisation’s readiness to deploy AI agents internally. The Agentic Readiness Score measures the outward side: how ready you are to be found and chosen by them. Read the full explainer

Why we switch web search off

With search on, an AI can look you up in the moment. With it off, it can only use what it already knows. A business named both ways is part of the AI’s knowledge. A business named only with search is easy to lose. The confidence ratio compares the two: close to 1 is strong.

  • Found, not knownNamed mostly when the AI can search.

What goes into the rating

Five parts, weighted for the kind of business you run. A law firm and an online store need different things from AI.

  • Technical access

    Can AI crawlers reach and read your website, or are they turned away?

  • Content clarity

    Can an AI understand, quote and summarise your content correctly?

  • Brand command

    Do AI assistants name you unprompted, and describe you accurately?

  • Structured data

    Are your products, prices and details published in a form machines can use?

  • Transaction readiness

    Could an AI agent enquire, book or buy from you on a customer’s behalf?

Questions

What is agentic readiness?

A business with high agentic readiness can be reached by AI crawlers, is described accurately, is named when customers ask for a recommendation, publishes machine-readable data, and can take an enquiry or order through an agent.

What is the Agentic Readiness Score?

A rating from 0 to 100 that measures how often AI assistants name your business when customers ask for a recommendation, how accurately they describe you, and how ready your website and data are for AI agents. Every rating is built from 19 customer questions asked five times each, with web search on and again with it off.

How is this different from SEO, GEO or AEO?

SEO measures where you appear in a list of search results. Generative engine optimisation (GEO) and answer engine optimisation (AEO) are practices for improving how AI answers use your content. The Agentic Readiness Score is a measurement: it tells you where you stand today, so you can see whether that work is paying off.

Which AI assistants do you test?

The assistants people use to research and buy, including ChatGPT and Google’s AI. We review the mix as the market changes, and every report states which assistants were tested.

What do I get?

Your rating and band, a breakdown across the five parts, your confidence ratio, how you compare with your market, and the changes most likely to lift your rating.

Can my rating change?

Yes. AI models are updated and the web changes. Each rating is a dated snapshot, so you can see it move as you make improvements.
