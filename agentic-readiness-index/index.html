The Agentic Readiness Index

A recurring benchmark of how visible well-known brands are to AI assistants, measured with the published Agentic Readiness method.

Updated 23 November 2026Published by The Optimisers

What the index measures

The index applies the Agentic Readiness method to a fixed cohort of well-known brands in each market. Every brand gets the same question set, the same repeat runs and the same web search comparison, so results can be compared across sectors and over time.

It answers questions that matter to boards, marketers and anyone reporting on AI: which brands AI assistants recommend by default, which sectors are falling behind, and how quickly that is changing.

How often it’s updated

Cohort brands are re-tested on a regular cycle and each round is saved as a dated snapshot. Published figures always carry the snapshot date and method version.

Published results

Index results are released with a summary of findings, sector breakdowns and the method version used. Journalists can request the next release under embargo.

Citing the index
