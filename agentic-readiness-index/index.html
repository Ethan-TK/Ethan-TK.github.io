The Agentic Readiness Index

A recurring benchmark of how visible well-known brands are to AI assistants, measured with the published method.

Updated 23 November 2026

What the index measures

The index applies the Agentic Readiness method to a fixed cohort of well-known brands in each market. Every brand gets the same 19-question battery, the same five repeat runs and the same web search comparison, so results can be compared across sectors and over time.

It shows which brands AI assistants recommend by default, which sectors are falling behind, and how quickly that is changing.

Updates

Cohort brands are re-tested on a regular cycle and each round is saved as a dated snapshot. Published figures always carry the snapshot date and method version.

Published results

Results are released with a summary of findings and sector breakdowns. Journalists can request the next release under embargo through the media page.
