Agentic Readiness Score methodology

How every rating is produced, published so results can be checked and cited.

Summary

Each rating combines five pillars. Four are measured from the business’s website and data. The fifth, Brand Command, is measured by asking AI assistants 19 customer questions, 5 times each, with web search on and off. Pillars are weighted for the type of business and reported as a score from 0 to 100 in five bands.

The five pillars

  1. P1 Technical Access. Whether AI crawlers and agents are allowed in, and can find every page.
  2. P2 Content Extractability. Whether your content can be read, quoted and summarised accurately.
  3. P3 Brand Command. How often AI assistants name you when customers ask, and how they describe you.
  4. P4 Data Liquidity. Whether your products, prices, services and details are machine-readable.
  5. P5 Transaction Readiness. Whether an AI agent could enquire, book or buy on a customer’s behalf.

The question set

A fixed battery of 19 questions, written the way customers talk to AI assistants, across three stages:

StageWhat the customer is doingExample form
DiscoveryWorking out what they need“I need help with X. Where do I start?”
EvaluationChecking out a specific business“Tell me about [business].”
TransactionChoosing who to buy from“Best [service] in [location].”

Questions are adapted to category and location; the same battery is used for every business in a comparison.

Repeat runs

AI assistants are slightly random: the same question can get a different answer each time. One run is a yes or no; five runs give a mention probability (“named in 4 of 5 runs” is 0.8). Five runs is the standard for published index results. More runs give tighter estimates at higher cost.

Web search on and off

Every question is asked with the assistant’s web search enabled and again with it disabled.

Confidence ratio = mention rate with web search ÷ mention rate without web search

A ratio of 1.5 or below is strong; above 1.5 up to 3 is moderate; above 3 is weak; no mentions without search means the business is search-dependent.

Brand Command

Brand Command combines the mention probability across all answers with sentiment: of the mentions, how many are positive. Mention probability carries the most weight.

Business-type weighting

The overall score uses one of six weighting profiles: e-commerce, professional services, local business, software and technology, media and publishing, and general (all pillars equal). The profile is stated in every report, and businesses are compared with others on the same profile. Profile weights are reviewed periodically; changes are recorded below.

Bands

  • Leading 80–100A default recommendation in its category.
  • Visible 60–79Regularly named and described accurately.
  • Emerging 40–59Known, but often passed over.
  • Low visibility 20–39Occasionally named, rarely recommended.
  • Invisible 0–19AI assistants don’t know it exists.

The free check

The free check runs 23 automated tests on a site’s home page, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap, covering P1, P2, P4 and P5. Each test passes (full credit), partly passes (half credit) or is missing (no credit), weighted within its pillar. The quick check score is a weighted combination of the four pillars: Technical Access counts most, because nothing else matters if assistants are locked out, followed by Content Extractability and Data Liquidity, then Transaction Readiness. Critical blockers also cap the score: a page with almost no text before JavaScript runs, a site that turns away every AI search crawler, a noindex instruction, or a site without HTTPS cannot score above 40 however well everything else is done, and the worst cases are capped much lower. It is not an Agentic Readiness Score and should not be reported as one.

Limitations

  • Ratings are snapshots. AI models and the web change; cite every rating with its test date.
  • Assistants differ. Results apply to the assistants tested, which every report lists.
  • Questions shape answers. A fixed battery keeps comparisons fair but can’t cover every phrasing.
  • Sampling error. Five runs give a reliable estimate, not an exact figure; don’t over-read small differences.

Glossary

Battery
The full set of 19 questions.
Brand Command
How often, and how favourably, AI assistants mention a business.
Confidence ratio
Mention rate with web search divided by mention rate without it.
Mention probability
The share of answers that name the business.
Repeats (N)
How many times each question is asked.
Snapshot
A dated record of a business’s results.
