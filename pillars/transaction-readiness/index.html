Why it matters

Assistants are starting to act, not just advise: booking appointments, requesting quotes and placing orders. This is agentic commerce. If an agent can’t see a clear way to transact with you, it will choose someone it can.

What the free check looks at

  • Clear next step. Links and buttons to buy, book, enquire or start.
  • Ways to get in touch. Phone links, email links and forms.
  • Prices visible. Prices or ranges in the page text.
  • Prices in structured data. Offers with price and currency.
  • Actions described for agents. schema.org actions such as ReserveAction or OrderAction.

In the full rating

The full rating also tests whether key flows can be completed, and which agentic commerce standards you support.

How to improve

  • Label your main actions plainly: “Book a consultation”, “Get a quote”, “Add to cart”.
  • Publish prices or ranges where you can.
  • Keep booking and checkout flows simple, with as few steps and logins as possible.
  • Describe available actions in schema with potentialAction.
