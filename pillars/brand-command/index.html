Why it matters

This is the outcome everything else feeds. When someone asks an assistant who to use, it names two or three businesses. Brand Command measures how often you’re one of them, and whether what it says is positive.

In the full rating

Brand Command can’t be read from your website. The full rating asks AI assistants 19 customer questions across discovery, evaluation and transaction, five times each, with web search on and off. It measures mention probability, sentiment and the confidence ratio.

How to improve

  • Make sure the facts about you are consistent everywhere: your site, profiles, directories and reviews.
  • Earn mentions on sites assistants trust, such as industry bodies, media and comparison sites.
  • Fix the technical and content pillars first. Assistants can’t recommend what they can’t read.
