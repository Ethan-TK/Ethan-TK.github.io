Why it matters

AI systems read your pages as text. If the content only appears after JavaScript runs, or isn’t organised into clear sections, an assistant may miss it or summarise you wrongly.

What the free check looks at

  • Readable without JavaScript. How much text is in the HTML before scripts run.
  • Page title and description. Clear, well-sized title and meta description.
  • Heading structure. One main heading and clear section headings.
  • Language declared. The page states its language.
  • llms.txt summary. A plain-text guide to your business written for AI tools.
  • Image descriptions. Meaningful images have alt text.
  • Canonical URL. The preferred address for the page is declared.

In the full rating

The full rating also checks how assistants actually summarise you, and whether those summaries are accurate.

How to improve

  • Render key content on the server so it’s in the HTML.
  • Break pages into sections with descriptive H2 headings that match questions customers ask.
  • Publish /llms.txt with a short description of your business and links to your most important pages.
  • Write a meta description that states plainly what you do and where.
