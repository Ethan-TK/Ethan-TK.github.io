Why it matters

Agents compare options using structured facts: what you sell, what it costs, where you are and how to reach you. Data locked in images, PDFs or loose page text is hard for them to use.

What the free check looks at

  • Structured data present. Valid schema.org JSON-LD on the page.
  • Organisation details. Name, url, logo, address, phone and profile links in schema.
  • Open Graph tags. Title, description and image for link previews.
  • Offers and answers described. Product, Service, Offer, FAQ or breadcrumb schema.
  • Contact details machine-readable. Phone, email or address in structured data.

In the full rating

The full rating also reviews product feeds and data beyond the home page.

How to improve

  • Add Organization or LocalBusiness schema with complete details.
  • Mark up products and services with prices using Product, Service and Offer.
  • For online stores, keep product feeds complete and current.
  • Add FAQPage schema for the questions customers ask most.
