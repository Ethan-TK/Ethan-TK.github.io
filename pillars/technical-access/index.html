Why it matters

Before an assistant can recommend you, its crawlers have to be able to read your site. Many businesses block AI crawlers without knowing it, through a security plugin, a CDN setting or a copied robots.txt. Others hide their main pages from search entirely.

What the free check looks at

  • Secure connection. The site loads over HTTPS.
  • robots.txt. A robots.txt file exists and states your rules.
  • AI crawler access. Nine AI crawlers, from ChatGPT search to Common Crawl, are checked one by one against your robots.txt.
  • Indexing allowed. Nothing on the home page tells crawlers not to index it.
  • XML sitemap. A sitemap is declared or published, so every page can be found.

In the full rating

The full rating also looks at authority signals, such as links from other sites, which affect how likely crawlers are to have found you.

How to improve

  • Allow search and user-request crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User and PerplexityBot, unless you have a specific reason not to.
  • Decide deliberately about training crawlers such as GPTBot and Google-Extended. Blocking them is legitimate, but models will know less about you over time.
  • Check your CDN and security tools for bot-blocking rules that override robots.txt.
  • Publish an XML sitemap and reference it in robots.txt.
