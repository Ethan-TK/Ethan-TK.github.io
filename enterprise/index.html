Agentic readiness for enterprise and agencies

Independent ratings for brand portfolios: how AI assistants find, describe and recommend each brand, how you compare with competitors, and what to fix first.

Built for teams answering to a board

When customers start asking AI assistants instead of search engines, the question from leadership is simple: do they recommend us? The Agentic Readiness Score answers it with a measurement you can repeat, compare and defend, built on a published method.

What you getWhy it matters
Portfolio ratingsA rating for each brand, product line or market, on one consistent method.
Competitor benchmarksThe same questions put to AI about your named competitors, so the comparison is like for like.
Category contextYour results placed against the Agentic Readiness Index for your market.
Dated snapshotsRe-tests show whether changes to content, data and site are moving the result.
PrioritiesA ranked list of the changes most likely to lift each rating, by part.

How an engagement works

  1. Scope. Agree the brands, markets, categories and competitors to rate.
  2. Tailor the questions. Adapt the 19-question set to your categories and locations.
  3. Test. Run every question five times, with AI web search on and off, and record every answer.
  4. Report. Ratings, bands, part breakdowns, confidence ratios and priorities for each brand.
  5. Re-test. Repeat on a regular cycle to track change.

Data and governance

  • No system access needed. We test public AI assistants with customer-style questions. We don’t need your analytics, systems or customer data.
  • Auditable. AI answers are recorded, so any finding can be traced to the responses behind it.
  • Published method. The method and its limitations are public, versioned and the same for every client.
