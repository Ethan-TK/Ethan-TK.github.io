Agentic readiness for enterprise and agencies

Independent ratings for brand portfolios: how AI assistants find, describe and recommend each brand, how you compare with competitors, and what to fix first.

Built for teams answering to a board

When customers ask AI assistants instead of search engines, leadership asks a simple question: do they recommend us? The Agentic Readiness Score answers it with a measurement you can repeat, compare and defend, on a published method.

What you getWhy it matters

How an engagement works

  1. Scope. Agree brands, markets, categories and competitors.
  2. Tailor the questions. Adapt the 19-question battery to your categories and locations.
  3. Test. Run every question five times, with web search on and off, and record every answer.
  4. Report. Ratings, bands, pillar breakdowns, confidence ratios and priorities.
  5. Re-test. Repeat on a regular cycle to track change.

Data and governance

  • No system access needed. We test public AI assistants with customer-style questions; we don’t need your analytics, systems or customer data.
  • Auditable. AI answers are recorded, so any finding can be traced to the responses behind it.
  • Test mode and safeguards. Testing runs with dry-run checks and spending caps before anything goes live.
  • Published method. Public, versioned and the same for every client.
