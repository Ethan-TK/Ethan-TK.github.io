About the Agentic Readiness Score

The Agentic Readiness Score measures how ready a business is for AI agents: whether assistants such as ChatGPT and Google’s AI find, describe and recommend it when customers ask. Each rating is built from 19 customer questions, each asked five times with AI web search on and again with it off, and is scored from 0 to 100. The score and the Agentic Readiness Index are published by The Optimisers, based in New Zealand.

Citing our data

Agentic Readiness Index, The Optimisers, [month year]. https://agenticreadiness.ai/agentic-readiness-index/
  • Include the snapshot date with any score; results change as AI models change.
  • Refer to bands as well as numbers when comparing businesses. Small score differences are within sampling error.
  • Examples on this website marked as sample data are illustrations, not results.
