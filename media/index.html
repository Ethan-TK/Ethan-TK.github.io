About the Agentic Readiness Score

The Agentic Readiness Score measures how ready a business is for AI agents: whether assistants such as ChatGPT and Google’s AI can find, understand, recommend and transact with it. It combines five pillars, including Brand Command, measured by asking AI assistants 19 customer questions five times each with web search on and off, and scores businesses from 0 to 100. The score and the Agentic Readiness Index are published by The Optimisers, based in New Zealand.
